# 🎧 JamPilot

**The one-click DJ app that never lets the party stop.**

JamPilot auto-mixes your music library into a seamless, nonstop set — complete with beat-matched transitions, smooth fades, and crowd-pumping effects. Whether you want to kick back and let the music flow or jump in and take control, JamPilot puts the decks in your hands.

---

## ✨ What JamPilot Does

- **One-Click Mixing** — Hit play and JamPilot takes over. It analyzes your tracks and builds a continuous mix automatically.
- **Beat-Matched Transitions** — Every track change is synced to the beat, so the energy never drops.
- **Smooth Fades & Crossfades** — Natural-sounding blends keep the vibe flowing from one song to the next.
- **Party Effects** — Build-ups, drops, reverb sweeps, and more — applied automatically at just the right moments.
- **Live Tweaking** — Adjust EQ, effects, crossfade timing, or the upcoming track queue on the fly.
- **Full Manual Control** — Take over the decks anytime. JamPilot steps aside the moment you grab the wheel.

---

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Import your music** — Connect your library (local files, Spotify, Apple Music, and more).
2. **Press Play** — JamPilot analyzes BPM, key, and energy levels across your tracks.
3. **Enjoy the mix** — Sit back while JamPilot curates and blends your set in real time.
4. **Tweak or take over** — Adjust anything live, or switch to manual mode whenever inspiration strikes.
